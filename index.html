The Dreamplays story

Dreamplays began with research and experimentation in 2026. Instead of treating a simple device as a limitation, the founder used it as a starting point for learning, researching and building on the web. The journey included learning HTML, CSS and JavaScript, studying digital tools and experimenting with real projects.

The first major public project became AYARTSTUDIO, a free drawing and learning platform. As the work grew beyond a single project, Dreamplays expanded into a team effort with web management, traffic operations, development and research.

The platform was publicly launched on July 1, 2026. Dreamplays HQ continues to develop educational and digital tools with a long-term goal: making useful learning technology available to more people without putting cost at the center.
