Dreamplays began with research and experimentation in 2026. OUR STORY

From a Simple Idea
to Dreamplays HQ

Every company has a beginning. Ours began with curiosity, learning, persistence and a decision to build something meaningful.

In June 2026, during the summer break, instead of spending the time only on vacation, the idea behind Dreamplays HQ began to take shape. Hours were dedicated to learning, researching and understanding how websites and digital products are built.

With only a mobile phone, research was carried out through Google, documentation, Reddit, LinkedIn and other online resources. The goal was simple: to understand whether it was possible to build something meaningful with limited resources, patience and consistent hard work.

There were many moments when giving up felt easier. Instead of quitting, the decision was made to keep learning — from HTML, CSS and JavaScript to web development, digital tools and online business.

“Instead of giving up, I chose to keep learning.”

That learning journey eventually led to the creation of Dreamplays HQ, with the vision of building useful, accessible and free educational tools for the next generation.

The first major project became AYARTSTUDIO — an art and drawing platform designed to help people explore, create and learn.

As the project grew and more features were planned, it became clear that building a larger platform required teamwork. That is when Yugank Vishnoi joined the journey as Co-Founder, helping with web management, checking and traffic management.

Later, Aaditya Kumar joined the team as Project & Web Developer, contributing to web development and helping identify and improve website issues.

Dreamplays HQ officially became publicly available on 1 July 2026. Since then, the project has welcomed 500+ visitors and continues to grow step by step.

Along the journey, the team has also received certificates from Google and Microsoft and developed collaborations with other creators and technology projects.

Today, Dreamplays HQ is more than just a website. It represents a simple belief: young people can learn, build and create meaningful technology when they are given the opportunity to try.
